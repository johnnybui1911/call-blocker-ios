# Store metadata drafts — AntiCall

Approved drafts for later. Nothing here has been entered in App Store Connect or Google Play Console.

The reasoning and the search data behind these strings are in
[reports/261128-1028-research-store-search-aso-for-app-name.md](reports/261128-1028-research-store-search-aso-for-app-name.md).

The name under the icon is always **AntiCall**. The store name is a separate field and differs by language.

## App Store

Limits: name 30 characters, subtitle 30, keywords 100. Counts are in brackets.

| Field | Vietnamese listing | English listing (US and UK) |
|---|---|---|
| Name | AntiCall: Chặn cuộc gọi rác (27) | AntiCall: Spam Call Blocker (27) |
| Subtitle | Đầu số spam, telesale, lừa đảo (30) | Block scam calls by prefix (26) |
| Keywords | `số,lạ,quảng,cáo,làm,phiền,điện,thoại,đòi,nợ,lọc,quấy,rối,ứng,dụng,app,tiếp,thị,cảnh,báo,bảo,vệ` (94) | `unknown,caller,number,robocall,telemarketer,filter,blacklist,phone,junk,stop,nuisance,fraud,range` (97) |

Rules these follow:

- No word is repeated across name, subtitle and keywords, because the App Store combines words from all three.
- Keywords are single words separated by commas without spaces. "số" and "lạ" combine with "chặn" from the name to match "chặn số lạ".
- "tin nhắn" and "ngoài danh bạ" are left out: the app does not filter SMS and cannot block every number outside contacts.

## Google Play (for the Android version)

Limits: title 30 characters, short description 80.

| Field | Vietnamese | English |
|---|---|---|
| Title | AntiCall: Chặn cuộc gọi rác (27) | AntiCall: Spam Call Blocker (27) |
| Short description | Chặn cuộc gọi rác, lừa đảo, telesale theo đầu số. Không cần tài khoản. (70) | Block spam and scam calls by number prefix. No account needed. (62) |

Long description: Google Play has no hidden keyword field, so use each of these phrases two to four times in natural sentences.

- Vietnamese: chặn cuộc gọi rác, chặn số rác, chặn đầu số, chặn cuộc gọi lừa đảo, chặn số lạ, cuộc gọi quảng cáo.
- English: spam call blocker, block scam calls, block number prefix.

Do not title the listing "Chặn cuộc gọi rác & lừa đảo"; an existing Android prefix blocker already uses exactly that.
