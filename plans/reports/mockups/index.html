C · Bàn phím

What is illustrative. Every number shown comes from three sample entries (028 888…, 090 123 4567, 024 7300…). In B, the count and capacity tiles carry a “Minh họa” badge because they are computed from that sample. No direction shows “calls blocked” figures: iOS does not report them, so the real app cannot either. “Mở Cài đặt” only flips the mockup to the on state, and swipe actions (Sửa, Bỏ chặn), backup and restore are not simulated.
