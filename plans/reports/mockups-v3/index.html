Chặn cuộc gọi: “Hợp nhất” in dark and light

One design, two modes. It keeps layout A “Lá chắn” and the micro-interactions of version 3 (animated shield, stamp and confetti on block, swipe to unblock) and takes its colour from the round-one mockup: vivid green for protected, vivid red for danger. Both phones are live; the prototype itself follows the system appearance and has a toggle in its top bar.

Platform notes

What is simulated. Numbers come from three sample entries, and nothing shows “calls blocked” figures because iOS does not report them. “Mở Cài đặt” only flips the mockup to the on state. Editing a number, backup and restore are not simulated. Tokens live in src/tokens.json; the build fails if any listed contrast pair drops below its target.
